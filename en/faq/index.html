Frequently Asked Questions

How long does a project take?

It depends on the project's size and complexity: a company website usually takes 5 to 10 working days, while complete systems and custom applications take 4 to 12 weeks. We give you a clear timeline with defined milestones before work begins — and we stick to it.

How do I start a new project with you?

Reach out through the contact page or book a free consultation. We discuss your idea and pin down the requirements, then send you a detailed technical and financial proposal within 48 hours. Once you approve, work starts immediately.

Which technologies do you use?

We use modern technologies: Next.js and React for front ends, Node.js with modern databases for back ends, plus advanced penetration-testing tools such as Burp Suite and Metasploit. We always choose the stack that best fits your project and its goals.

How do you ensure the quality of delivered projects?

We follow a strict methodology with weekly code reviews, automated and manual testing (unit & E2E), and a full security review before delivery — plus a staging environment where you follow progress and review each phase before we move on.

Do you sign non-disclosure agreements (NDAs)?

Yes. We sign an NDA before any detailed meeting, all client data is kept strictly confidential over encrypted channels, and your name or project details are never mentioned anywhere without your prior written permission.

What does the post-delivery warranty cover?

The warranty covers free fixes for any software defects for 30 days after delivery, along with technical support by email and direct contact to make sure the project runs fully stable in production.

What payment methods and instalment terms are available?

We accept bank transfers and online payment gateways. Projects use a flexible instalment plan: 40% at kickoff, 30% when the first version is delivered, and 30% at final delivery. Small projects are split 50/50.

Do you issue official invoices?

Yes. We issue an official tax invoice for every payment, sent to you electronically right away, and can provide any additional documents your company needs for accounting and auditing.

What is the refund or cancellation policy?

You can cancel a project at any time; the work actually completed up to the cancellation date is billed and the remaining balance is refunded automatically within 7 working days — with no hidden fees or complicated conditions.

Do you offer technical support after delivery?

Yes. Every project comes with 30 days of free technical support after delivery, after which flexible monthly support contracts are available, covering continuous monitoring, updates and rapid response to emergencies.

What does the monthly maintenance contract include?

It includes regular automatic backups, package updates and security patches, round-the-clock performance and security monitoring, a detailed monthly report, and extra development hours you can use when needed.

How do follow-up meetings work during the project?

We hold a weekly video call to show the progress made and the next phase, followed by a written report, and you can track tasks in real time on the project management board at any time.
